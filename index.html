Our Story

A small shop, run the right way.

Alexandria Glass started the way most good shops do. One truck, one phone line, and a promise that the work would be done right the first time.

Today we’re nine deep, with our daughter Caroline running the front of house alongside Leslie, and a crew of installers who’ve forgotten more about windshields, storefronts, and shower glass than most folks will ever learn.

We’re not the cheapest. We’re not the biggest. We’re the shop you call when you want it done once, done right, and you want a real person to pick up the phone when you call.

Randy Estes

How it Works.

No. 03 — Process
Three steps from "got a chip" to "back on the road."
  1. i

    Call or Stop By

    Tell us what you've got: year, make, model, dimensions, or just bring it in. We'll quote you on the spot.

  2. ii

    We Schedule You

    Most auto glass is same-day or next-day. Custom shower glass runs 5 to 7 business days. We confirm everything before you commit.

  3. iii

    Done Right

    You drop off, or we come to you. Installed by hand by the same crew that's been doing it for years. Warranted for life of the part.
